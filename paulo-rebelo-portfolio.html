AI Delivery Framework·In practice at Sephora

Agentic SDLC: governing AI-assisted software delivery.

A framework for bringing AI agents into the software development lifecycle — from requirements and design through implementation, testing, release, and continuous improvement. The focus is clear delegation, accountable human decisions, and measurable delivery quality. I'm leading this shift at Sephora with a 5-person team.

Make work ready for AI

Provide explicit requirements, acceptance criteria, relevant business context, and engineering conventions so agents operate within a defined scope.

Keep people accountable

Combine automated checks with human review of architecture, code, business acceptance, and release readiness. Match autonomy to task risk and demonstrated reliability.

Learn from delivery

Distinguish AI-assisted work in delivery measurement. Review quality signals and agent usage, then improve context, estimation, and team practices through feedback.
